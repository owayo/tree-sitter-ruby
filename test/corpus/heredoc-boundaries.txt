==================
interpolation 直後の行途中の終端語を本文に残す
==================

x = <<EOS
#{1}EOS
EOS

---

(program
  (assignment left: (identifier) right: (heredoc_beginning))
  (heredoc_body
    (heredoc_content)
    (interpolation (integer))
    (heredoc_content)
    (heredoc_end)))
